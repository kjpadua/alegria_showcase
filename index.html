About Alegria

Alegria is a coastal town in Cebu known for waterfalls, scenic views, and local culture. This page showcases attractions, food, lodging, events, and tips for your visit.

  • Province: Cebu
  • Region: Central Visayas
  • Language: Cebuano & Filipino
  • Best time to visit: November to May (dry season)

Local Food

Lechon Cebu

World-famous roasted pig specialty of Cebu.

Puso

Steamed rice wrapped in woven coconut leaves.

Danggit

Small, salted, and sun-dried rabbitfish loved by locals.

Ngohlong

Spring roll filled with ground pork and shrimp, seasoned perfectly.

Kinilaw

Raw fish cured in vinegar with onions, chili, and ginger.

Balbacua

Rich, slow-cooked beef stew made with oxtail and tendons.
